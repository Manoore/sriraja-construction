Process

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We walk the plot, check documents and arrange a soil test.

  2. 2

    Drawings and approvals

    Architectural and structural drawings, then the building permit through Telangana's BuildNow portal for GHMC or HMDA limits.

  3. 3

    BOQ and contract

    An itemised bill of quantities, material brands in writing, and payments tied to stages.

  4. 4

    Construction

    A site engineer on every job, with progress photos and a site update each week.

  5. 5

    Handover

    Snag list cleared, as-built drawings and warranties handed over, with help on the occupancy certificate.
